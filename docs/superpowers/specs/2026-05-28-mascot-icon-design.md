# Mascot Icon Design

## Goal

Create a premium, minimal, iconic product mark inspired by the existing owl mascot without copying the mascot illustration directly.

## Direction

Chosen direction: `geometric mask`.

The icon should feel:

- iconic before illustrative
- cute without becoming childish
- premium and compact
- recognizable at small sizes

## Source DNA From The Mascot

Keep only the irreducible visual cues:

- pointed owl ear silhouette
- large frontal gaze
- small red beak accent

Discard:

- full body
- belly
- wings
- feet
- books
- graduation cap
- facial micro-details

## Mark Construction

The final symbol is a new frontal owl-mask glyph built from simple geometry:

- a single compact outer silhouette
- two oversized eye fields
- a small centered diamond beak
- strong bilateral symmetry

The silhouette should read as an owl first, while the internal spacing gives it a modern product-icon feel similar to high-recognition AI app marks.

## Color

- deep navy background
- warm cream for the face and eyes
- crimson red accent for the beak

The palette preserves continuity with the mascot while making the mark feel more premium and durable.

## Success Criteria

- works at favicon scale
- still looks distinct in monochrome
- does not read like a simplified mascot sticker
- feels original and brandable
